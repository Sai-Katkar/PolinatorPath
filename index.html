What this prototype can and cannot tell you

Parks are mapped as habitat nodes for demonstration; this does not verify that each park supports pollinators. Candidate sites are hypothetical and do not indicate available land. The travel range, straight-line distances, network links, and connectivity score are assumptions for a simplified planning exercise, not ecological measurements or predictions.
